### 1. Add New Paper (manually)

**Design Concept & Benefits**  
This feature allows you to manually add any research paper or reference into your knowledge base. The purpose is to:  
- **Personal Knowledge Management**: Centralize scattered information in one searchable system.  
- **Avoid Forgetting**: Ensure important information is not lost or forgotten.  
- **Boost Learning Efficiency**: Easily retrieve existing paper records anytime.

**Steps**  

**Step 1:** Open edit mode to start adding a new paper. You can fill in the details manually or use AI autofill to save time.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/addNewPaper-manually/step1-openEditMode.png" alt="Open edit mode to add a new paper" width="100%"/>

**Step 2:** Click the "Add New Paper" button to create a new entry.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/addNewPaper-manually/step2-clickHere.png" alt="Click the add new paper button" width="100%"/>

**Step 3:** Fill in the paper information. Choose manual input or AI autofill according to your preference.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/addNewPaper-manually/step3-fillPartialInfo-AIFill.png" alt="Fill in paper info manually or use AI autofill" width="100%"/>

**Step 4:** Review and save the paper to add it into your knowledge base.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/addNewPaper-manually/step4-noPromblem-save.png" alt="Save the new paper successfully" width="100%"/>

### 2. Create Pillars (manually)

**Design Concept & Benefits**  
Pillars are high-level categories that help you organize papers by discipline, topic, or research area:  
- **Structured Management**: Quickly find all papers in a certain domain.  
- **Learning Planning**: Understand your coverage in each field.  
- **Team Collaboration**: Everyone in the team can have a consistent view of the main directions.

**Steps**  

**Step 1:** Return to the homepage to start creating new Pillars.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/createPillars-manually/step1-returnToHomepage.png" alt="Return to homepage to start creating pillars" width="100%"/>

**Step 2:** Scroll down to the Pillars section to see existing pillars or add new ones.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/createPillars-manually/step2-scrollDown.png" alt="Scroll down to pillars section" width="100%"/>

**Step 3:** Open edit mode to add or modify pillar information.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/createPillars-manually/step3-openEditMode.png" alt="Open edit mode for pillars" width="100%"/>

**Step 4:** Click "Add New Pillar" to create a new pillar.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/createPillars-manually/step4-clickHere.png" alt="Click add new pillar" width="100%"/>

**Step 5:** Enter the pillar name, description, and research area.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/createPillars-manually/step5-clickToEnter.png" alt="Enter the pillar details" width="100%"/>

**Step 6:** Review and edit the research area and description to make it clear and understandable.

- **Better Visualization**: Makes your knowledge structure easier to understand.

### 5. Share Knowledge Base

**Design Concept & Benefits**  
Sharing the knowledge base allows syncing across devices or collaborating with a team:  
- **Data Synchronization**: Avoid duplicate work on multiple computers.  
- **Team Collaboration**: Multiple users can work on the same knowledge base.  
- **Backup & Safety**: Prevent data loss.

**Steps**  

**Step 1:** Click "Share Knowledge Base" to start.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/shareKnowledgeBase/step1-clickHere.png" alt="Click to share the knowledge base" width="100%"/>

**Step 2:** Save the current database.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/shareKnowledgeBase/step2-saveDB.png" alt="Save the database" width="100%"/>

**Step 3:** Choose a location to save it locally.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/shareKnowledgeBase/step3-chooseAsaveLocaltion.png" alt="Choose location to save" width="100%"/>

**Step 4-5:** The system automatically links related entries to maintain internal relationships.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/shareKnowledgeBase/step4_1-auto_linked.png" alt="Auto-link step 1" width="100%"/>
<img src="../assets/guide_images/shareKnowledgeBase/step4_2-auto_linked.png" alt="Auto-link step 2" width="100%"/>

**Step 6:** Open the saved database file to check its contents.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/shareKnowledgeBase/step5-openDBfile.png" alt="Open saved database file" width="100%"/>

**Step 7:** Choose the file to load into the system.  
<img src="../assets/guide_images/shareKnowledgeBase/step6-chooseFileToOpen.png" alt="Choose file to open" width="100%"/>

**Step 8:** The system will automatically sync the local copy with the database.
